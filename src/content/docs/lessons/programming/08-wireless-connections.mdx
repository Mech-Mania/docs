As of so far, to make any changes to the operation of motors, servos, or other components, we have had to re-flash the code to our microcontroller. For the purposes of driving a robot, especially in a competition format, this becomes infeasible extremely quickly. Instead, we can pass data to the microcontroller wirelessly through a variety of means.

### Radio

`Insert radio stuff here`

Though both ESP32 and Arduino have similar methods of handling radio transmission with [RF24](https://nrf24.github.io/RF24/), their usage of other wireless connections diverge drastically; Arduino does not have native Wifi and Bluetooth support, and relies on external boards such as the ESP8266, wheareas the ESP32 has built-in support for bluetooth, as well as both regular and connectionless WIFI.
